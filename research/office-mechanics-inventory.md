## Separation of mechanics and cognition

The [Conscription mechanics](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/offices/conscription/mechanics.md) explicitly split deterministic functions—validate a commission, instantiate a substrate, install an exact Profile, package/deliver artifacts—from cognitive qualification decisions. Delivery was not Seat occupancy or deployment.

The [Senate mechanics](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/offices/senate/mechanics.md) similarly listed recording, sealed packet validation, sterile witness instantiation and enforcement as mechanical operations, leaving substantive questions, findings and disposition to attributed cognitive roles. A containment/readiness defect prevented starting the hearing.

## Generalizable architectural insight

The reusable distinction is **who may decide** versus **what mechanically enforces and records the decision**. Separation does not automatically imply distinct AI agents, Offices, daemons, database tables or services. Revisit those only on observed need.

## Supersession warning

Historical PRs modified responsibility boundaries, and the Office-first reset removed older executable architecture. Do not project the final August Office map onto earlier experimental phases or today's MWS. Cross-compare branches and individual PRs before declaring a design lineage complete.
