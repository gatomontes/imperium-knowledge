# Constitutional principles — distilled

**Status:** Historical doctrinal proposals worth retaining; not automatically active rules for the new Minimum Working Sample.

**Primary source:** [Imperium Doctrine](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/imperium-doctrine.md).

## Principles

1. **Assume nothing.** Every introduced object, role and mechanism should identify the observed need, assumption and evidence that justify it.
2. **Behavior precedes institution.** Observation → explicit assumptions → evidence → decision → outcome → revision. Do not require an Office merely because an Office appears in a map.
3. **Authority is not ability.** A model's tools, credentials, runtime access or output text do not create permission to use them. Authorization must have an identifiable source, scope, holder and expiry/revocation.
4. **Governance is enforced outside the agent.** Agent self-compliance is insufficient for consequential operations. Identity, authority and effect must be correlated.
5. **No evidence is not approval.** Missing, stale, ambiguous or conflicting evidence must not become a positive disposition by default.
6. **Distinct states stay distinct.** Drafted, reviewed, approved, authorized, executed, qualified, deployed and completed are different claims.
7. **Preserve provenance across transformations.** Handoffs and summaries should not silently change ownership, uncertainty, substance or permissions.
8. **Failures should be bounded.** A local failure should stop the affected operation, not invent authority or automatically imply global collapse.
9. **Treat revision as reassessment.** Material changes can invalidate prior claims, qualifications or authorization.
10. **Demand falsifiable language and neutral evidence.** Labels such as `trusted` or `secure` require precise tests, limitations and corroboration.

## Deferred theoretical mechanisms

The [runtime concurrency/replay contract](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/contracts/runtime-concurrency-replay.md) describes transaction binding, reservations, single-use commissions, idempotency and replay ledgers. These are **not prerequisite components of a two-agent synchronous MWS**. Reconsider them when real state-changing or concurrent workflows justify the complexity.

The [MasterMason](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/mastermason.md), [Imperator](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/imperator.md), and bootstrap contracts specify a later institutional runtime proposal, not the initial multi-agent framework.
