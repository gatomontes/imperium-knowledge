Cumulative, source-linked knowledge from Imperium experiments and designs. **This repository is not an executable specification or an implementation repository.**

## Evidence conventions

Each document links to its source in `gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02` or `gatomontes/imperium-symfony`. Classify every claim as **doctrinal proposal**, **documented implementation**, **documented test**, **observed live result**, or **future hypothesis**; do not confuse these. A previous doctrine's internal label `constitutional-root` reflects its status *in that old design*, not automatic authority over future work.

## Preservation

Do not delete or rewrite the source repositories as part of extraction. Repository history, abandoned ideas, and failed experiments are historical evidence, not automatically authoritative requirements.
