# Agent and artifact lifecycle distinctions

**Classification:** Historical doctrinal design. Preserve concepts without importing its institutions into the fresh MWS.

## Identity and artifact taxonomy

The [constitutional root](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/imperium-doctrine.md) distinguishes:

| Term | Meaning in former doctrine |
| --- | --- |
| Persona | Canonical cognitive source / identity and disposition |
| Profile | Immutable versioned, purpose-specific derivative with qualification contract |
| Manifestation | Exact instantiated operative/runtime identity |
| Authorization | Permission for a specified holder/action/scope, distinct from ability |
| Office | Jurisdiction owner |
| Seat | Localization of institutional authority |

This is an **analysis vocabulary**, not a demand to create six PHP classes before an agent can run.

## Separation of responsibilities

- [Foundry](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/offices/foundry/doctrine.md) proposed production and static adversarial review of an exact Persona candidate.
- [Senate](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/offices/senate/doctrine.md) proposed live confirmation of a precisely bound manifestation (model, settings, harness, Persona version and trials); examination success was not admission.
- [Garrison](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/offices/garrison/doctrine.md) proposed Persona admission, custody and retrieval; custody did not imply authorization for a mission.
- [Laboratorium](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/offices/laboratorium/doctrine.md) proposed deriving versioned target-specific artifacts from a Persona; derivation was not installation, qualification or deployment.
- [Conscription](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/offices/conscription/doctrine.md) proposed assembly/qualification of operative manifestations against exact contracts.

## Lifecycle evidence

The [Profile contract](https://github.com/gatomontes/imperium-agentica-v02/blob/main/contracts/profile-artifact.md) separates immutable artifact contents from append-only approval/current-active/revocation attestations. Design value: a version must be identifiable, and success of one kind should not be substituted for authorization of another kind.

## Scope note

Automatic bootstrap, formation ceremonies, institutional jurisdictions, and qualification hearings are **deferred designs**. In the MWS, configure two agents manually and observe their interactions. Introduce richer lifecycle concepts only in response to demonstrable needs.
